Hotel Information, Q&A & Policies

Customer Service Q&A

Q: What are the check-in and check-out times?

A: Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in is subject to availability.

Q: How does the cancellation policy work?

A: Guests can cancel or alter their booking free of charge up to 3 days before their designated arrival date.

Q: Is breakfast included with reservations?

A: Complimentary breakfast buffet is included for Executive and Presidential suites, and available as an add-on for others.
